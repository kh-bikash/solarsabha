# Hard questions, honest answers
- **Isn't this a calculator with a chatbot?** The calculator is 10% of it. The value is auditing real quotes, running a collective decision, and following up for weeks, which needs documents, judgement and waiting.
- **Who are the vendors?** In the demo, simulated personas, labelled. The reading and auditing work on real PDFs.
- **How do you know the rules are right?** Every rule is in a dated rulebook with a source and a status. Anything not from an official document is marked "verify" and shown as a warning.
- **What if the AI is wrong?** Numbers come from code. The AI cannot send, sign, pay or file. Humans approve all messages. "Not verified" is an allowed answer.
- **Prompt injection?** Documents are data. We scan for instruction-like text, ignore it, and flag it. The agents have no harmful tools to misuse.
- **Why not use a vendor's free report?** The vendor is selling. This works for the society.
- **Why multiple agents?** Small focused jobs, testable separately. The Planner is plain code, not an agent, on purpose.
- **Real impact?** kW installed and tonnes of CO2 avoided (CEA factor), plus days saved. For the hackathon these are computed from a sample society, not measured outcomes.
